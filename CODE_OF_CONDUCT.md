# Code of Conduct

Creator Engine is a governance substrate. Its contributor community
is expected to behave in a way that is consistent with that goal:
clear, evidence-based, respectful, and accountable.

## Our standards

We expect everyone participating in Creator Engine — contributors,
maintainers, ratifiers, and users — to:

- Be respectful and professional in all project spaces (issues, pull
  requests, discussions, reviews, and any associated channels).
- Engage with technical disagreement on the merits, citing specs,
  code, evidence, and the canonical document set rather than personal
  attributes.
- Assume good faith, ask before assuming intent, and prefer the most
  charitable reasonable reading of a contribution.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and the work; do not use
  project spaces for harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or
  personal attacks.
- Respect the project's ratification boundaries: do not pressure
  maintainers or ratifiers to bypass governance, security, or
  redaction requirements.
- Respect privacy: do not publish private communications, personal
  data, secrets, tenant-identifying material, or vulnerability
  details in public project spaces (see [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md)).

The following behavior is not acceptable in any project space:

- Harassment, threats, or sustained personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct targeting personal characteristics.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, reviews, or governance
  processes.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Attempting to coerce ratification, merges, releases, or any
  privileged operation outside the authority model.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — the repository,
its issues and pull requests, any project-affiliated discussion or
chat venues, and public communication where an individual is
representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you observe behavior that you believe violates this Code, please
report it privately to the project maintainers. A dedicated conduct
contact will be published before public launch; until then, use
whatever private channel you currently have for reaching the
maintainers, or wait for the published contact rather than escalating
publicly. Placeholder: `contact to be published before public launch`.

When reporting, please include what happened, where it happened,
approximate dates/links if available, and any context that would help
maintainers understand the situation. Reports will be handled with
discretion. Reporters will not be retaliated against for good-faith
reports.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including
private warnings, requests to edit or remove content, temporary or
permanent restrictions on participation in project spaces, or removal
of contributions. Severe or repeated violations may result in
permanent removal from the project community.

This document is informed by widely used community codes of conduct
(such as the Contributor Covenant) and may be revised as the project
matures.
